Why Tesel

Made for the worst day.

In an emergency, the network is the first thing to fail: overloaded masts, power cuts, valleys with no coverage. Just when a team most needs to know about its people. Tesel is made so that this does not stop it.

  • For anyone

    Free and with no sign-up: it opens in the browser, you type a name and that is it. No email, no phone number, no password.

  • Understood at once

    Made to be used in a hurry and under stress: in a couple of minutes you know how it works, with no manual or training.

  • Not tied to one network

    It uses the internet when there is one and radio when there is not. What you write offline goes out as soon as there is a way.

  • The team's, for the team

    Everything is encrypted with a key only the group has. Nobody else can read it, not even us.

Resilient communications

A second way, by radio.

Radio does not replace the internet: it backs it up. It is another channel that does not depend on phone masts or the power grid, and Tesel uses it by itself, without anyone having to change anything.

  1. With coverage

    Like any messaging app: over wifi or mobile data, through the server, which passes everything on encrypted without being able to read it.

  2. Without coverage

    Each phone pairs over Bluetooth with a pocket radio. The radios pass messages on to each other, from one to the next, kilometres apart.

  3. Both at once

    A phone with a radio and coverage acts as a bridge with nothing to set up: what comes by radio goes up to the internet and the other way round. So whoever has no coverage stays in the loop.

  • Everything travels by radio: SOS calls, messages, positions, marks on the map, tasks and reports.
  • The urgent first: radio carries little data, so an SOS goes ahead of messages, and messages ahead of positions.
  • The more radios, the further: each one repeats what it hears. From a kilometre in a city to tens between high points.
  • No licences, no operators: the free 868 MHz band in Europe, and radios that use very little power.

What it does

One shared picture of the situation.

Every member sees the same thing, on the same map, at the same time: where the team is, what is happening and what has been decided.

  1. The team on the map

    Where everyone is, their state and what they carry. Each person decides when to share where they are.

  2. SOS with acknowledgement

    A call for help reaches the whole team with its position. Whoever calls sees who has received it, and the others can be guided there.

  3. Chat and reports

    Short messages, dictated too, and situation reports with the casualties, the hazards and what is needed.

  4. Tasks and sweeps

    Jobs for people or teams, with their place on the map, and search sweeps split into strips.

  5. The ground marked

    Sectors, meeting points, routes and danger areas that warn whoever walks into them.

  6. Offline maps

    The map of the area, downloaded before setting out, to see it in detail offline.

Start now

Up and running in a minute.

It works in the browser of any phone, tablet or computer. No app stores, and it updates itself.

  1. Open Tesel

    Go to tesel.eu/app and type your name. To keep it at hand, add it to your home screen from the browser's menu.

  2. Create the group

    The coordinator creates it and invites the rest with a QR code or a link. Whoever opens it is in.

  3. Get ready to lose the network

    Download the map of your area on wifi and, if you have radios, pair them with your phones. And practise on a quiet day.

Privacy

What happens in the team stays in the team.

  • No sign-up

    No email, no phone number, no password. Each member only picks a name.

  • End-to-end encryption

    Only the group holds the key. The server cannot read anything.

  • Your position, when you choose

    It is only shared if you choose, and you stop sharing it with one tap.

Questions

Before you start.

Does it cost anything?

No. Tesel is free and needs no sign-up. You would only pay for radios, if you use them.

Do we need radios?

No. With coverage, Tesel needs nothing else. Radios are plan B for when the network fails: best to have them ready beforehand.

Does everyone need a radio?

No. Whoever carries a radio stays in touch even without coverage. Whoever does not gets what came by radio as soon as they have coverage, through the bridges.

Which radios work?

LoRa radios with MeshCore's “Companion Bluetooth” firmware. We are testing them in the field and will publish the recommended models here.

Does it work on an iPhone?

Yes. To pair a radio over Bluetooth on an iPhone, open Tesel in the Bluefy browser, as Safari does not allow it. It is a stopgap we are still testing.

What if a town council wants its own network?

That is what we are preparing Tesel Professional for: repeaters, a dedicated server, training and maintenance.
